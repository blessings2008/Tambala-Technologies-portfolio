What we manage

A consistent presence, managed with purpose.

The exact scope is agreed with each client. Depending on the arrangement, social media management can include:

Account management

Day-to-day care of agreed social media accounts, profiles and publishing workflows.

Content planning

Plan themes, content calendars and publishing schedules around your business and audience.

Post creation

Create social media posts and supporting copy that communicate your services, updates and brand clearly.

Scheduling and publishing

Prepare and schedule approved content so your pages maintain a more consistent rhythm.

Community engagement

Monitor and engage with comments and messages, and help keep conversations moving.

Performance reporting

Review available performance information and report on activity, audience response and opportunities to improve.

How it works

Clear planning. Consistent execution.

1. UnderstandLearn about your business, audience, goals and existing accounts.
2. PlanAgree on content themes, responsibilities, platforms and a publishing schedule.
3. Create and managePrepare content, schedule approved posts and monitor agreed channels.
4. ReviewShare performance reports and identify practical improvements.

Organic management and paid advertising are separate.

Regular account management and organic content are scoped separately from paid advertising. Paid campaigns can be added when appropriate, but advertising budgets and platform ad spend are agreed in advance and are not included automatically in the management fee.

Frequently asked questions

Social media management questions.

Which platforms can you manage?

We can discuss the platforms your business already uses and identify which channels make sense for your audience and goals.

Do you create the posts?

Yes. Post creation and supporting copy can be included in the agreed content plan and service scope.

Do you reply to messages and comments?

Community engagement and inbox or comment monitoring can be included. Response responsibilities and escalation rules are agreed with the client.

Are paid adverts included?

No. Paid advertising is optional and separate from organic management. Ad spend is agreed in advance and funded separately.

How do we get started?

Contact Tambala with your business details, social media pages and what you want to improve. We can then discuss a suitable scope.
