Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Tambala Technologies may receive when you use our website or contact us, and how we use it.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Tambala Technologies is a technology business based in Ntcheu, Malawi. This policy applies to information collected through https://tambalatechnologies.site and information you choose to send to us through the contact details published on the website.

1. Information you provide

When you submit the website project enquiry form, we may receive your name, email address, optional phone or WhatsApp number, selected service, optional estimated budget and project message. If you contact us directly by email or WhatsApp, we receive the information and message you choose to share in that conversation.

2. How we use information

We use enquiry and contact information to respond to you, understand and discuss a project, prepare or follow up on a proposal, provide requested services, and maintain appropriate business records. We do not ask you to submit passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through the website enquiry form.

3. How website enquiries are handled

The website enquiry form sends the information you submit to our server so that it can be forwarded to our business email using the Resend email delivery service. The website application is not currently designed to save enquiry submissions in its own customer database. Email providers and infrastructure providers involved in delivering or hosting communications may process information as part of their services and under their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, deliver communications or provide a service you request. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users or business.

5. Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was received, including handling an enquiry, managing a business relationship or meeting applicable record-keeping obligations. Messages sent by email or WhatsApp may remain in those services until they are deleted under the relevant account and provider settings. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Links to other services

Our website may link to third-party services, including WhatsApp and social media platforms. If you follow those links or communicate through those services, their own privacy policies and terms apply. We do not control how third parties process information on their platforms.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have sent to us or to request that we correct or delete information where appropriate and legally possible. We may need to retain some information for legitimate business or legal reasons. To make a request, use the contact details below and describe the information or conversation concerned.

8. Children

Our website and project enquiry form are intended for general business and service enquiries, not for collecting personal information from children. Please do not submit a child's personal information unless you are authorised to do so.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or information-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
