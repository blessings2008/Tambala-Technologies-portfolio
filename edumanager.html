The product direction

School operations and learning, connected.

The planned platform brings the people, information and everyday work of a school together. Its scope is being developed around the needs of administrators, teachers, students and parents or guardians.

School administration

A central workspace for school operations, organisational records, user access and day-to-day administration.

Students and academics

Connected student, class, subject, attendance, assessment and results workflows, designed to keep academic information consistent.

Teaching and learning

Digital learning is part of the platform's core direction, connected with teachers, students, classes and subjects rather than bolted on separately.

School finance and resources

A planned operational area for managing relevant school financial and resource workflows as the product scope is implemented.

Staff and communication

Support for the people who run and use a school, with role-aware access and clearer communication between school stakeholders.

Insight and reporting

Structured school information intended to support useful reports and better visibility into academic and operational activity.

Designed as a platform

Built for more than one school.

EduManager is being architected as a multi-school platform from the outset. Each school is intended to operate in its own protected environment, while the platform supports different schools and their distinct needs.

Flexible access through subscription tiers

The product direction uses subscription tiers to activate the modules and features available to each school. This is intended to let institutions choose a suitable level of capability without turning the platform into disconnected products. The exact packages and availability will be defined as development progresses.

Who it is for

One platform. Different school roles.

Administrators

Coordinate school operations, manage access and view the information needed to oversee the institution.

Teachers

Work with their authorised classes, subjects, students and learning activities.

Students and families

Access relevant learning and school information through role-appropriate experiences.

Development status

EduManager is actively being developed. The current preview uses sample data; it is not a live school deployment, and the full operational modules are not yet generally available. We will share confirmed capabilities and release information as implementation and testing progress.
